I design for the gap between what people say and what they actually do.

I’m Harsha Dhore, a design researcher and MDes candidate at IIT Delhi, with a background in fine arts. I work on digital experiences for users who are usually designed around rather than designed for.

My approach is research-led: understand the context first, then design for the behaviour that already exists. I combine extended field research with structured behavioural frameworks, and I test with the people the product is actually for.

stated behaviour

What users tell you in an interview — shaped by memory, intention and a wish to be helpful.

observed behaviour

What they actually do in context — shaped by constraints, habit and the people around them.
