Sensitivity: If frequency uplift is only +1 (half), total = ₹6.52 Cr. If churn cut is 15 pts, total = ₹11.53 Cr. Try the sliders to see where the bet is robust.
How we kept it honest: Baseline uses 6 orders/yr (survey showed 5–6 per 4 months ≈ 15–18/yr). Frequency +2 is below the +3.4 seen in the “clear ETA” pilot. Churn cut claims only anxiety-driven churn (∼40% of 35%). Support saving counts only “Where is my order?” contacts at verified ₹45 cost.
Methodology: Retention gain = (MAU × segment% × target% × churn-reduction-pts) × (orders/yr × commission). Frequency gain = (MAU × segment% × target%) × (uplift × commission). Support gain = (MAU × segment% × target%) × (tickets/yr × reduction% × cost/ticket). All figures are annual, India ₹.
